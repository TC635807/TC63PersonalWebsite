FAQs
How do I get started with EdgeOne Makers?
Getting started with EdgeOne Makers is very simple. Just go to the Makers Console, connect your Git Repository, select our templates or your own Git Project, and then click deploy. Follow the on-page guidance to quickly bring your project online.
What types of applications can I deploy?
Makers supports deploying a variety of types of applications, including but not limited to:
Static Site: Websites built using static generators like Astro and Hexo.
Single-Page App(SPA): Applications developed based on modern front-end frameworks such as React and Vue.
Full-Stack App: Full-stack applications built using Next.js and other full-stack frameworks or Functions to meet your needs for high performance and flexible backends.
Agent App: Intelligent applications built on mainstream Agent frameworks such as Claude and LangGraph, meeting the needs of conversational assistants, programming assistants, and other scenarios.
