Plans and prices for publishers of all sizes. Best-in-class features.
Disqus offers flexible, customizable ad formats that drive meaningful revenue growth via exclusive inventory on your site. We work with you to find creative and optimized implementations that preserve the user experience.
We offer 30-day free trial periods for Disqus Comments Plus, Disqus Comments Pro, and Polls Pro. Submitting a valid payment method is required to begin a free trial. You can cancel anytime during your free trial period. At the conclusion of the free trial period, you will be charged the monthly or annual fee for the plan you selected.
We accept all major credit cards.
If you are a small, nonprofit site, you may be eligible for a free subscription to our Comments Plus plan. You can learn more by emailing publisher-success@disqus.com.
Disqus Boards is exclusive to Business tier publishers. If you're on a Plus or Pro plan and interested in adding Boards to your site, please contact us at publisher-success@disqus.com and our team will be happy to discuss your options.
You can switch to a new plan at any time by going to the Subscription & Billing page in your account. Select the plan you’d like to switch to and immediately start using new features.
You can cancel your Disqus subscription at any time by emailing cancellation@disqus.com.
